# Distribution principle

The remarkable part should be the work itself, not a gimmick.

The format should therefore be:

**Reverse job ad → evidence-first CV → timestamped proof → live repositories.**

A recruiter can understand the pitch in 30 seconds and verify it in five minutes.

That is faster and more credible than a conventional multi-page CV full of unsupported adjectives.

7. Submit to Google / Google DeepMind second.
8. Record submission timestamp and target.
9. Begin broader distribution immediately afterward.

No external submission should be claimed as completed without a confirmation, application receipt, sent-email record, or equivalent evidence.
